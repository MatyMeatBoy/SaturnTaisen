# Tactical mode pairing (TS11): maps, units, items

Offline work, no runtime build. The 542 PC SPR candidates of tactical mode are 177 `SAKURA2/MAP`, 291 `SAKURA2/UNIT`, 57 `SAKURA1/ITEM` and 17 `SAKURA1/MAP` (strategy-map screens). This note records the evidence per block; the data is names, sizes and hashes only (no pixels). Regenerate with `tools/tactical_pairing.py`; tests are `tests/test_tactical_pairing.py` (synthetic, no game data).

Status words: `paired` (firm evidence), `ambiguous` (several candidates), `no counterpart` (nothing established or nothing decodable on the Saturn side). A paired map is `paired-not-wired` in the register until the runtime has a fingerprint and a sampler for it.

## Maps (block 1)

Data: `config/hd-tactical-map-pairs.json`. Command:

```powershell
python tools/tactical_pairing.py maps --pc <folder with the extracted PC SAKURA1/SAKURA2> --iso1 "local/prepared/disc1/Track 01.iso" --iso2 "local/prepared/disc2/Track 01.iso" --out config/hd-tactical-map-pairs.json
```

Method: every Saturn `/SAKURA2/MAPnn.BIN` (13 files on disc 1, 14 on disc 2, 27 in total) is compared by SHA-256 with `SAKURA2/MAP/MAPnn/MAPnn.BIN` of the PC edition. The two editions ship the same terrain file, so this is stronger than a luminance signature: all 27 are byte-identical. The PC `MAPnn.TAB` (a text file) then names the ground SPR. It is `Ground:` for 11 maps and `UnderGround:` for 16 (MAP02, MAP06 and MAP21 among them); the existing verified grounds (MAP01, 02, 04, 06, 21) were identified the same way. The luminance route of `match_hd_all.py` is not used here: it needs the Saturn ground rendered from the VDP2 page, which is what the runtime already does for the verified maps.

Result: 27 of 27 Saturn maps are `paired` to a PC ground SPR. Six distinct grounds are now `hd` in the runtime (MAP01 patch 0102, MAP02, MAP04, [MAP05](map05-pc-ground.md), MAP06, MAP21, with the same MAP01 file on disc 2). The other 21 move from `unknown` to `paired-not-wired` in `config/hd-chapter-coverage.json` (generator change in `tools/hd_chapter_coverage.py`). Not paired: PC `MAP27` has a BIN and a TAB (it reuses the MAP06 files) but no Saturn file.

Two ground layouts matter for the runtime:

- single image (2048x2048, 2048x1280, 1392x960 ...): the Stage01/Stage02 sampler design applies;
- mosaic of equal tiles (MAP03 626 tiles of 64x64, MAP04 629 tiles, MAP05 46 tiles of 256x256, MAP20 96 tiles of 128x128; MAP23 holds four 1392x1232 images): an assembler is needed first. MAP04 and MAP05 use the runtime placement assembler.

The remaining comparisons need live pattern-name fingerprints and ground registration. The two below now have a live disposition. Wiring one map at a time is the open row TW6b.

## Units (block 2)

Data: `config/hd-tactical-unit-inventory.json` (names, sizes, SHA-256, FNV-1a, image counts and sizes; no pixels). Command: `python tools/tactical_pairing.py units --pc <folder> --iso1 ... --iso2 ... --out config/hd-tactical-unit-inventory.json`.

Inventory: 27 Saturn `/SAKURA2/UNITnn.BIN` files (13 on disc 1, 14 on disc 2; UNIT01 is on both discs) hold only 10 distinct contents (UNIT02, 03, 06, 21 are identical, as are UNIT04, 09, 10, and UNIT14, 16, 18, 20, 23-26 on disc 2); each starts with an offset table (first word `0xD0`) followed by data that no decoder in `tools/` reads. The PC side is 291 SPR files in 44 groups (`PLAYER/<character>`, `PL_AT`, `PL_SP`, `GATTAI`, `BOSS`, `BOSSAT`, `BOSSSP`, `MAPOBJ`, `COMMON`, `EFFECT`, ...), organised per character, not per stage.

Result: 0 paired, 0 ambiguous, 318 `no counterpart`. Reason: a luminance pairing needs the Saturn unit frames decoded first, and the stage bank is not a frame picture the existing decoders understand; also the Saturn unit art is probably not only in `UNITnn.BIN` (other `SAKURA2` banks such as `Nnn.BIN`, `Knn.BIN`, `Hnn.BIN`, `Gnn.BIN`, `GKnn.BIN`, `FIXnn.BIN` and `GATTAI.BIN` are candidates). That is a lead, not a finding. No statuses in the register change (the 26 `tactical-units` entries stay `unknown`), so `config/hd-chapter-coverage.json` and `docs/HD_PROGRESS.md` are not regenerated by this block.

Not verified: any relation between a Saturn bank and a PC character folder; the lead about the other banks was not opened. Next step is row TS11a.

## Items (block 3)

Data: `config/hd-tactical-item-inventory.json` (names, sizes, SHA-256, image counts; no pixels). Command: `python tools/tactical_pairing.py items --pc <folder>` (it reads `config/hd-item-map.json` and `config/hd-item-review.json`; no disc needed).

The Saturn side was paired before this work: `tools/match_hd_items.py` (32x32 luminance signature correlation, 112x112 Saturn card against the 224x224 PC picture) and the eye review. This block adds the PC-side view of the 57 `SAKURA1/ITEM` SPR files. A PC picture is `paired` when a Saturn item is confirmed to it (review `confirmed`, or tier `content` without a review row), `ambiguous` when it is only the best candidate of items the review rejected or called icon-only, and `no counterpart` when no Saturn item ranks it first.

Result for the 57 PC pictures: 41 paired, three ambiguous and 13 without a counterpart. The Saturn side has 41 confirmed files and 39 rejected. ITEM03, ITEM12 and ITEM27 now join to the full cards, following the [item-card review](pc-menu-hud-inventory.md#item03-item12-and-item27). The inventory records the pairings, and the coverage register records the renderer evidence.
