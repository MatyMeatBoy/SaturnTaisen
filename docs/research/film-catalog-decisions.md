# Film catalog decisions

The prepared Saturn discs contain byte-identical copies of `SAKURA1/OPSAKURA.CPK` (SHA-256 `34f33e33d9292b6e54ccb32b800d89f96f7ce9f4ce7905e85be7093046601ef0`). FFprobe identifies one 320 × 224 Cinepak video stream lasting 0.146667 seconds, without an audio stream. Its opening frame is nearly white with pink ornamentation. The catalog treats both copies as the native transition.

On PC, `MOVIE118.MPG` and `MOVIE015.MPG` have identical bytes (SHA-256 `2b4c08d17ed8a364b103495c45440ebe7f47423d837d6d2e335de84113df28ac`). `MOVIE015` already serves the corresponding Saturn film on both discs. The FAD generator records the redundant name as an alias and preserves the canonical runtime table. `REDLOGO` is the remaining unmatched PC file and is outside Saturn film replacement.

The alternate PC edits of MOVIE004, MOVIE009, MOVIE011, MOVIE020, MOVIE102 and MOVIE105 stay native under the existing timing decision. There are 44 connected PC clips among 50 distinct mapped films, with the six native choices recorded explicitly. The status describes the existing runtime and does not certify listening, all in-game transitions or package inclusion.
