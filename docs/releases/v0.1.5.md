# v0.1.5 · Checkpoint toward v0.2

November 17, 2026. Preliminary version for Windows x64; it does not certify the complete game.

## Available changes

- Original/Enhanced selector in the launcher and when starting the game, with Sakura cards. It can also be changed from settings.
- Widescreen composition in recognized scenes: video enlarged to the largest integer that fits; dialogue, portraits, and protruding elements kept as a set. Unknown scenes use the original composition.
- Twenty generated decorative Saturn variants, still preliminary, with the corresponding theme's palette and integer scaling. Decorative excess may be cropped; the content keeps its proportions.
- Video shadow tied to its new rectangle, and backgrounds that follow the game's color offsets during fades.
- In-game menu with a Sakura theme and interface text at window resolution. Esc/F1 and Start+Select keep access to settings.
- Optional crisp English text for recognized glyphs, keeping original advance, position, and ink box. Requires Consolas installed on Windows; that font is not distributed. Other fonts or texts keep the original pixels.
- The interactive launcher closes after the game's first confirmed presentation. If it fails before being ready, the launcher keeps the error.
- Disk-read caching on the host, without modifying commands or waits of the emulated CD. Backgrounds are prepared when their theme is needed and kept cached.

## Validation

Full suite: 58 tests passed. Includes AOT boot with no game interpreter, saves, import, incremental generation, selector/composition, disk cache, PNGs allowed in the package, and a real night scene. Synthetic VDP2 tests verify blending, windows, and color offsets with bank and sign selection. Real video and conversation captures at 1280×720 were inspected.

The cache produced identical hashes over 1,800 frames: 959 requests were resolved with 39 host reads, versus 959 without cache. This does not certify an equivalent reduction in the game's load time nor does it eliminate its CD waits.

## Pending and limits

- Full campaign, all battles and minigames, audio fidelity, and real automatic disc transition. Discs can be selected manually.
- RetroAchievements, physical M30 tests, and expanded controller and save compatibility.
- Running the PC version, adapting the English scripts, and an HD pack for its assets. 2,059 SPRED were inventoried and their 19 decorative backgrounds decoded; their generated extensions remain preliminary in the repository, not presented as a playable PC mode.
- More visual review per scene and resolution, and automatic generation of real examples for new installs. Without local examples, the cards show a schematic of the proportions.

No discs, BIOS, videos, saves, or extracted original assets are included. The package only incorporates the recorded and verified generated backgrounds, plus the program, tools, reproducible patches, and licenses. v0.1.0 is kept as an independent release.

## Use and update

Unzip into a new folder and open `SaturnTaisenFirst.exe`. Import your discs and, for English, use your copies patched with the Saturn v2.1 translation. Save a copy of your previous version's `user/` folder before moving it to the new package. The previous installation is not automatically altered.
