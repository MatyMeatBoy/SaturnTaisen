# v0.2.14-alpha

- Enhanced adds the Iris and Ogami combo cards and sharper frames for Options, System, Controls, Inventory and Load. Load arrows and rails remain visible when the renderer reuses VDP2 lines.
- Tactical status artwork loads off the presentation thread. The sampled opening of the MAP12 status panel spent 2.69–3.72 ms in its first presentation field, compared with 6.33–8.84 ms before. The HD artwork appeared one field later in that sample.
- Windows now supports Unicode paths for extracted PC resources, including folders with accents or non-Latin characters. The tactical panel and an edited movie matched the same replays from ASCII paths.
- Natural disc 2 play was checked for three more scrolling backgrounds, including their native scroll and fallback. Spanish opening scroll and Enhanced combo, room selection and timed dialogue remain part of the release checks.

Use your own Saturn discs and optionally the PC edition for HD resources. The recorded comparisons cover the stated replays and samples.
