# v0.2.4-alpha — HD scene pictures and character bodies, episode cards in 16:9, Spanish cards (CANDIDATE, not published)

Candidate built and checked locally on 2026-11-19; **nothing is tagged or uploaded**. Package folder: `local/releases/SaturnTaisen-v0.2.4-alpha-win64` (about 1.2 GB, layout `SaturnTaisen.exe` + `data/` + `roms/`). Built by `tools/package_release.py` with the runtime `local/build/sakura/sakura.exe` (patches 0001-0071), the launcher and helpers from `local/build/v024-package-binaries` and the video layers from `local/work/taisen-parches/v0.2`.

## What is new

- **HD scene pictures (T74).** The PC picture replaces the Saturn scene picture under the native overlays (459 pictures, recognised by the palette fingerprint of the picture layer; character cut-outs and anything with English text baked in are excluded). Needs the PC edition in the HD pack.
- **HD character bodies with faces (T19).** A body recognised by the hash of its VDP1 texture is drawn from the PC body; its eyes and mouth are drawn from the matching PC parts (exact file layout where it fits, otherwise a match by shape or lip colour). Seen in game: `NC97`, `NC82`, `NC20`, `NC11`, `NC55` and `NC30` (the last with a native face). A face frame with no clear match stays as the native sprite, so the expression is always right.
- **HD portraits follow every step of the white fade (patch0057)**, no more fall back to the Saturn portrait in between.
- **Choice text no longer flickers** for a frame when the highlight moves (patch0058), the **Zoom box** needs a geometry to persist six frames before it moves, and the HD text is 6 % smaller in its cell (Spanish text was cramped).
- **Episode title cards fill the 16:9 window (patch0071)**, from the picture's own edges, no asset.
- **Spanish:** the ten episode cards and the LOADING scroll (CARGANDO) are program-made drafts inside the Spanish layer (`parches/espanol-v0.1-disc1.xdelta` regenerated; the other layers are unchanged).
- Tools: Saturn picture decoders (`tools/decode_saturn_bg.py`: backgrounds, full-screen pictures, item pictures, bodies), mass pairing with the PC pictures, eye reviews in `config/`.

## Checked

- Guest state is untouched: identical per-frame hashes against v0.2.2 over 900 to 6,400 frames on five scenes, 0 interpreted blocks.
- A 2,500-frame HD/native comparison of `NC20` (random input): the HD expression agrees with the native one in every captured frame; a 32-capture fade with a body on screen shows no artefacts.
- `preparar-juego.exe prepare` from the Japanese discs on a copy of the package: every step passes, and the Spanish disc 1 is byte-identical to the one the cards were built into (SHA-256 `f3294095...`).

## Not verified / limits

- The runtime was not played through; only saved scenes. Bodies not seen in any saved state are untested. Fade-in of a body is unchecked.
- Cards and CARGANDO are drafts in a different face from the original lettering; a painted version is still wanted (T87). The widening of the cards is a plain extension with faint banding at one edge.
- Mouths that cannot be matched stay native (a small pixelated patch on the HD face); `NC30` keeps its native face.
- Scrolling and battle backgrounds, HUD and menus (T54), MPEG videos from the PC edition (T53) and emulation speed (T86) are not part of this candidate. The frame time is unchanged (slow frames are emulation, VDP about 7 ms and SH-2 about 6.5 ms).
- Debug environment variables (`SATRECOMP_BODY_TRACE`, `SATRECOMP_LAYER_TRACE`) are compiled in; they do nothing unless set.
