# v0.2.5-alpha — HD videos from the PC edition, 16:9 episode cards, faster VDP and SH-2

Built and checked locally on 2026-11-19 (runtime `local/build/sakura/sakura.exe`, SDK patches 0001-0079). Layout `SaturnTaisen.exe` + `data/` + `roms/`, as in v0.2.4-alpha. The package never contains discs, BIOS or saves; the launcher prepares everything from your own files.

## What is new

- **HD videos (patches 0073, 0074, 0078).** If your PC edition has its `MOVIE` folder, the launcher copies it into the HD pack and the PC clip (MPEG-1 + MP2) replaces the Saturn film in the picture: 45 of the 50 clips (the six alternate cuts keep the Saturn film). The clip is identified by the start sector of the game's CD play command (`config/film-fad-map.json`), decoded on a worker thread with `pl_mpeg` (MIT) and follows the game's own frame count, so the guest is untouched. The PC audio replaces the Saturn film's sound while the clip plays.
- **16:9 episode title cards (patches 0071, 0076, 0079).** The ten cards fill the window. With the generated side art in `art/screen-backgrounds/` the card and its sides form one 398x224 picture that fills a 16:9 window; the sides blend with the card through its own mirrored edge. Without the art the sides are the card's mirrored edge. An optional finished wide card per episode (`art/cards-wide/TITLEn.png`) replaces both.
- **Speed (patches 0075 and 0077).** A pixel-identical speed-up of the VDP drawing and a faster SH-2 dispatch (about 1 ms per frame less in the SH-2 phase), both with the guest hashes unchanged.
- Tools and data for the next HD steps: FILM sector to MOVIE map, chapter shards (`docs/SHARDS.md`), `tools/integrate_prs.py`, `tools/check_patch_stack.py`.

## Checked

- Guest per-frame hashes identical with the clips on and off over the opening-film replay (6,201 frames, `interpreted 0`), and identical to the v0.2.4 build with the video, card and speed patches.
- Opening film: MOVIE001 shown sharp in the Zoom frame at 1280x720 and 1920x1080; cost about 0.27 ms per presented frame at 1080p.
- Episode 1 card at 1920x1080: sides and seam seen.
- Package: runtime hash identical to the tested executable; generated art listed in the package gate.
- `preparar-juego.exe prepare --language es` on a copy of the package, from the Japanese discs and the PC edition: every step passes (discs 1 and 2 in Japanese, English and Spanish) and the PC edition's `MOVIE` folder (52 MPEG files) is copied into `hd-resources/pc/MOVIE`, the folder the runtime reads the clips from. The prepared copy was not played (the clips were checked earlier with the same file layout).

## Not verified / limits

- **The PC clip audio has not been heard at normal speed**: the test runs were hidden and unthrottled. Sync with the Saturn film is within about one second, not frame-exact.
- Clips verified in game: the opening film and a second clip (cold boot) on disc 1; disc 2 clips, resume after a skip and every other clip are not.
- Only the episode 1 card was viewed with the generated sides; the other nine cards and the Spanish cards are untested in the runtime.
- The six alternate-cut clips keep the Saturn film.
- Patch 0080 (restoring the Saturn title audio after skipping a PC film) is on `main` but **not in this build**: after skipping a film the title music may stay silent.
- Still not HD: HUD and menus, the tactical mode, scrolling and battle backgrounds, most character bodies (only a handful of faces were seen), NC30/NC20 native faces. See `docs/PENDIENTES.md` and `docs/SHARDS.md`.
- The Spanish layer is unchanged from v0.2.4-alpha (the cards and CARGANDO are still program drafts, T87).
- Debug variables `SATRECOMP_CLIP_TRACE`, `SATRECOMP_BODY_TRACE`, `SATRECOMP_LAYER_TRACE` are compiled in and do nothing unless set.
