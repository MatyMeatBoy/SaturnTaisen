# v0.2.6-alpha — RetroAchievements, final episode cards, more HD pictures and bodies, a native-play build

A base release: everything integrated since v0.2.5-alpha (SDK patches 0080 to 0107), built and checked locally on 2026-11-19. Layout `SaturnTaisen.exe` + `data/` + `roms/`, as before. The package never contains discs, BIOS or saves; the launcher prepares everything from your own files.

## What is new

- **The game is played naturally.** Save states, load states, quick states (F5/F9), the reset point (F2/F3) and the slot keys (F6/F7) are switched off in every package (`release.marker` beside `sakura.exe`, patch 0097). The game's own saves work as always.
- **RetroAchievements (patch 0106, game 16546).** An `rcheevos` client reads the emulated memory after each confirmed frame, shows unlock popups with the achievement badge outside the dialogue and the native picture, keeps unlocks in a journal that survives a restart and syncs them when online, and the launcher has a login page (user name and token, the password is never stored or logged), the connection state and a log of what was sent. The `rcheevos` licence texts are in the package notices. **Not verified with a live account**: tests used a synthetic service; official condition compatibility, natural disc switching and live sync need a real RetroAchievements account.
- **Episode title cards (patches 0081, 0085, 0086, 0095; Codex).** The approved English and Spanish 16:9 cards are the only Enhanced presentation; Original keeps the Saturn card. The older strip and mirror code and the old art are gone.
- **More HD scene pictures.** Pictures whose palette is shared with another are told apart by the hash of their cell data (patch 0091, 33 more pictures); 7 reviewed `EVE` event pictures and 15 disc 2 `BBG` battle backgrounds join the scene table (patches 0094, 0104); the EVE202 tactical inset (0099), the horizontal pan of `BG2533` (0098), the pause screen `IC01` (0107), the PC ground of `Stage01` (0102) and the inventory cards and brackets (0090, 0093) draw in HD. The scene table now holds 481 pictures.
- **Large bodies.** `NC20` and `NC30` use their verified face maps (117 frames, patch 0089); the 129 bodies of the table are tried whenever they appear.
- **Video.** Clips resume from a saved state (test builds, 0096), the disc 2 opening plays in its native theatre (0103), the PC audio no longer cuts the title music after a skip (0080) and the picture/sound offset can be traced (0083).
- **Dialogue text** starts one native pixel lower so its top is not against the box (patch 0092).
- **Speed.** An in-place wait detector in the SH-2 dispatch (patch 0084).
- Tools for the next steps: chapter jump for testing (`tools/chapter_jump.py`), the autopilot coverage campaign (`tools/coverage_campaign.py`), shard claims with a 30-minute rule, `tools/integrate_prs.py` and `tools/rebase_patch.py`, and a coverage register that separates implemented from verified.

## Checked

- Guest per-frame hashes identical to the v0.2.4 reference over the 6,201-frame opening-film replay, with 0 interpreted blocks, on the full stack of 101 patches.
- Each integrated change brought its own A/B replays (hashes identical with the feature on and off): IC01 at 1440p (1,000 hashes), the cards at 1080p and 1440p, `BG2533`, `Stage01`, the disc 2 opening, NC20/NC30 faces (7,700 hashes), the inventory cards (656 hashes), RetroAchievements (230 hashes).
- With `release.marker` beside the executable, `--state-load` is refused; without it (development), it works.
- Opening film: the PC clip draws in the Zoom frame; shared-palette pictures `BG1011`, `BG1012`, `BG1050`, `BG1053`, `BG1100` and `BG1101` are recognised in 501 matches over the saved states.

## Not verified / limits

- **Seen in game:** over 358 saved states only 7 of the 129 bodies and 16 distinct scene pictures were ever drawn; no `EVE`, `BBG` or `IC` other than `IC01`. The rest is implemented (in the tables) but not yet seen: that is what the coverage campaign is for.
- PC clip audio not heard at normal speed; disc 2 clips beyond the opening and resume on disc 2 not verified.
- The six alternate film cuts keep the Saturn film by decision.
- Not HD yet: most menus and the HUD, scrolling backgrounds (`BG2252` and the other tall and wide pictures need a per-picture geometry), the other `IC` pictures, the tactical mode.
- RetroAchievements: see above (no live account test).
- The Spanish layer text is unchanged (the Spanish cards come from the Codex lettering layers).
- Debug environment variables (`SATRECOMP_CLIP_TRACE`, `SATRECOMP_BODY_TRACE`, `SATRECOMP_LAYER_TRACE`, `SATRECOMP_CELL_DUMP`) are compiled in and do nothing unless set.
