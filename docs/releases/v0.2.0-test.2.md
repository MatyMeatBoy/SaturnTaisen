## Deliveries

The public release contains the program, DLLs, code/patches, and allowed generative art. It requires the user's discs; it does not include discs, BIOS, scripts, saves, or original PC assets.

The authorized personal ZIP adds the owner's English disc 1 v2.1 and only the already-processed HD assets. It includes the Saturn videos contained on that disc; PC videos are not yet integrated. It is extracted into a new folder and Jugar.bat is used. It requires no installation of Python, MSYS2, SDK, or an external BIOS. The final unified product will require both discs.

Saves and settings are stored in user/. Scene and battle shortcuts use separate test backups. Do not copy an old portrait mod over hd-resources/ or delete saves to update. Physical RX6900XT/M30 testing is the target machine's responsibility.
