# v0.2.0-test.1 — presentation and battle test

Development checkpoint for testing on another PC. It does not certify the complete chapter 1 or campaign and does not replace previous packages.

## Included changes

- Fixed overlap of tactical submenus in Enhanced: Move, Atk, Def, Info, and End keep native graphics and selection with a separate frame.
- Widescreen Stage01 with a PixelGen v1 strip anchored to the world and sprites that cross the viewport boundary. Maximum 16:9, integer scale; Original is preserved.
- Extensions for the opening, title, status, and interlude; day/night maps. The LOADING screen has a provisional extension whose mosaic seam still needs polishing.
- Consistent command/texture captures for HD, dialogue row adjustments, and an M30 XInput profile documented by the owner.
- Local AOT extension covering the hang observed in Sumire's attack. Old packages are not modified.
- Dialogue catalog tools and portrait audit, shared queue, and technical/artistic handoff.

## Block tests

Twenty-five frontend fixtures passed. Three presentation runtime regressions passed, including the attack/camera path and limiting the visual change to the menu rectangle. The worker's tests compared 2,965 per-frame hashes in menus and battle, with zero interpreted blocks; four additional transitions compared 3,750. These hashes cover the StateHash contract, not every serialized field. Packaging, index, and exporter tests verify their limits separately.

Details and limits are in the `docs/research` reports. The final result of the suite and of the portable transfer is added when closing the delivery, without attributing to the target team a test that has not yet been done.

The broad suite ran 119 tests: two cache-count expectations became outdated when the preloaded screen backgrounds were added. The scope was fixed to the KABE narrative caches and the six presentation tests were re-run: 6/6 passed, including corruption/regeneration. The other 117 passed in the broad run. The two new translation-workspace tests also passed. That initial run is not presented as a failure-free suite.

## What is missing

Victory/exit and continuous playthrough of episode 1; campaign/disc 2 coverage; HD text for LIPS choices and an integrated preloaded catalog; pending portraits/animations and VDP2 blends; complete PC bodies, HUD, and videos; RetroAchievements. The Spanish patch is work in parallel and is not included as a finished translation.

## Two separate packages

The public release contains the program, DLLs, own fonts/patches, and allowed generative art. The user supplies their own discs. It does not contain dumps, BIOS, extracted scripts, saves, or a PC installation.

The owner's private ZIP additionally contains only their English disc 1 v2.1 and their already-processed HD assets, with test shortcuts. It is not uploaded to GitHub. The full folder is extracted and `Jugar.bat` is opened; it requires no Python, MSYS2, SDK, or external BIOS. The final unified product will require both discs. Testing on the target's RX 6900 XT and M30 hardware is the owner's responsibility.
