# PC decorative background extensions

All19 native WALL variants have preliminary generated masters. Palette/layout corrections and runtime integration are pending. Source references are locally decoded owner-provided PC SPRED bitmaps, not the Saturn screenshots. Original references are excluded from distribution.

Day generation brief: extend the native 640×480 Sakura Taisen PC cherry-blossom background horizontally to 16:9; preserve flat pink five-petal silhouettes, loose petals, lime/yellow/cream/peach diagonal gradient, flower density and fine ordered bitmap dithering. Keep the center calm for overlays. No UI, border, text, new objects, blur, noisy paper, faceted petals or glossy effects. Requested logical target 864×486; preserve the original central composition without stretching.

Night edit brief: use the generated day composition as the layout reference and native PC KABE0101 as the palette reference. Keep the flower/petal placements fixed and apply the dark navy, blue/lavender/purple gradient and teal/cyan motifs of the night reference. Preserve fine PC detail and ordered dithering.

QA limitation: the generation reinterprets portions of the central bitmap; exact source-pixel preservation was requested but is not established. Both outputs remain preliminary. The master dimensions are not the logical runtime grid; conversion to the PC grid and integer-only presentation require integration and visual validation. Do not load these through the Saturn 416×234 palette normalizer.

The same extension brief was applied separately to native PC trumpet lilies, hydrangeas, lily of the valley, morning glory, cosmos, chrysanthemums, plum branches, camellias and falling petals. Their night variants use the generated corresponding day arrangement plus the native PC night palette reference. No night variant exists in the source for KABE1000.
